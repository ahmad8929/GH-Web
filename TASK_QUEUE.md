# School Marketplace Website Queue

## Vision
Build a complete school marketplace platform for students, parents, schools, and tuition/coaching institutes to buy, sell, exchange, or donate education-related items.

2. Parent
3. School
4. Tuition/Coaching Institute

## Portal Structure
1. General marketplace
2. Used textbooks portal
3. Used uniforms portal
4. Charity and donation portal

## Required User Flows
1. Discover products through search, categories, filters, school matching, and location.
2. View product details with condition, price, school compatibility, class, subject, and seller details.
3. Save items, contact sellers, manage listings, and track activity.
4. Onboard with a simple role-first sign-up flow.
5. Move through cart and checkout for paid items.
6. Donate reusable educational items through a dedicated portal.

## Required Admin Flows
1. Manage users, schools, categories, listings, reports, approvals, transactions, banners, campaigns, and settings.
2. Search across users, schools, categories, and listings.
3. Filter, sort, paginate, export, and bulk manage listings.
4. Moderate reports, fraud, and dispute workflows.
